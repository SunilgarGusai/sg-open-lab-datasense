Calculation methods and limitations

Quantiles are Hyndman–Fan Type 7 (linear interpolation). Population and sample variance are shown separately using a numerically stabilized algorithm; sample variance requires at least two observations. Missing and invalid values are excluded, never treated as zero. The grouped frequency distribution depends on the chosen number of bins.
